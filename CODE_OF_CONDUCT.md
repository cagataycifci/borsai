# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project respectful, harassment-free, and welcoming regardless of identity, background, experience, or ability.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Give and accept technical feedback in good faith.
- Focus criticism on code, design, evidence, and outcomes—not people.
- Protect private data and credentials.
- Correct mistakes transparently.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discrimination, doxxing, sexualized conduct, deliberate disruption, publication of private information, or knowingly deceptive technical claims are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainer through an appropriate private GitHub channel. The maintainer may edit or remove content, close discussions, reject contributions, or temporarily or permanently restrict participation. Reports will be handled as confidentially as practical.

This policy applies in project repositories, discussions, issue trackers, pull requests, and project-related public spaces.
